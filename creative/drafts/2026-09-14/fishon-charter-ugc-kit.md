# DRAFT - Fish On Business: Local Charter/Guide UGC Ad Kit - 2026-09-14
Status: DRAFT for review, $0 lane. Scripts/storyboards only - no generation spend.
Buffer Blaster is the production seat (owner's family split); Fish On supplies
authority + niche. Claims rules: no guaranteed-catch language, no fake testimonials,
client supplies their own real trip footage/photos for final ads.

## Validation
- Competitor exists: charterprolaunch.com sells AI highlight videos to charters
  (trip photos -> shareable videos). That validates budget. GAP: they edit the
  operator's own footage into recaps; nobody sells UGC-style ADS (hook-driven,
  platform-native, spokesperson-led) plus the landing page to catch the click.
- Demand context: charters live on seasonality + word of mouth; Texas Gulf Coast
  guide density is high (Galveston/Freeport/Corpus/Port Aransas/Matagorda).

## 5 vertical ad concepts (9:16, 15-30s, phone-legible)

C1 "The 5 AM question" - HOOK: dock at dawn, text "What does $X actually get you on a
   Texas charter?" (price only with client's real number, else cut the line). BEATS:
   what's included (gear, bait, ice, cleaning) shown as real shots. PROOF: client's
   real catch photos with date. CTA: "Dates are on the calendar - link in bio."
C2 "First-timer" - HOOK: "Never held a rod? That's most of our trips." BEATS: guide
   coaching a beginner, the first catch moment (client footage). PROOF: beginner
   holding their fish. CTA: "Bring the kids. We handle the rest."
C3 "Local knowledge" - HOOK: "This jetty looks empty. It isn't." BEATS: guide reads
   water/wind/birds, explains the decision (educational). PROOF: the drift that paid
   off (real clip). CTA: "Fish with someone who reads this water daily."
   NOTE: education-forward, zero catch promises - the guide's judgment is the product.
C4 "The honest day" - HOOK: "Some days the fish win. Here's what we do about it."
   BEATS: honest talk about conditions, rescheduling, working harder. PROOF: mixed
   bag of real days. CTA: "Ask us anything before you book." Trust play - converts
   skeptical buyers burned by overpromising (see Fishbrain pain P8 analog).
C5 "Season opener" - HOOK: "Redfish season is here" (only when factually true at
   publish time - seasonality must be live-checked). BEATS: what changes this season,
   what to expect. PROOF: this week's real trip. CTA: "Weekend slots go first."

## Charter landing page template (Bar B skeleton, charter fill)
Badge: "Licensed Texas guide - [Port]" -> Hero: "[Guide name] puts you on Texas
water" + real photo -> 2 CTAs: "Check open dates" / "What's included" -> Two-sides:
TRIP (what's provided) | YOU (what to bring) -> Spotlight: real trip gallery (client
photos, dated) -> 4 cards: boat+gear / what we target (seasonal, factual) / safety+
license / how booking works -> 3 paths: family first trip / experienced angler /
group+corporate -> 4 steps: pick a date -> we confirm weather -> meet at the dock ->
fish -> Closing CTA -> Footer: TPWD license number, insurance note, no-catch-guarantee
honesty line ("We can't promise fish. We can promise the work." - the anti-slop
positioning from the BB pressure test, adapted).
