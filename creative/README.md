# Creative District

The design house of the city. Owner agents: **Darya Designs** and **Synthia Superdesign**
(repos: executiveusa/synthia-superdesign; Figma MCP connected). Every creative app, product
page and website must pass the bar here - Creative District is the design gate, not an
optional reviewer. Reviews run through the gauntlet loop: builder vs independent critic,
MATCH/NOT MATCH with specifics, numbered rounds, "live" never means "approved".

Automations in this district are OFF by default. Nothing publishes without owner approval
and an independent SHIP verdict.

## Design inheritance law

Creative District preserves quality standards across the portfolio. It does **not** preserve
visual identity across unrelated brands.

Permanent law:

> **Preserve standards. Forget style. Every brand earns its own world.**

Before material visual design begins, create a Brand World Contract using
`design-briefs/_BRAND-WORLD-TEMPLATE.md` and judge it against
`BRAND-WORLD-STANDARD.md`.

The required order is:

```text
product truth
  -> brand world contract
  -> design brief
  -> design spec
  -> build
  -> structural/accessibility/performance proof
  -> independent Gauntlet
  -> portfolio collision verdict
  -> SHIP / HOLD
```

Global usability, accessibility, ADHD-friendly clarity, verification, performance, truthful
telemetry, and craftsmanship may be reused freely. Unrelated typography, color behavior,
composition, image language, motion, hero structures, cards, whitespace patterns, and other
surface identity may not be inherited merely because they worked on another project.

Project-specific benchmark instructions remain project-specific. A directive to follow a
particular wireframe or reference for one product must not silently become a reusable house
template for other brands.
