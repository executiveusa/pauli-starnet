What the studio does

Four things, done properly.

01A calendar that thinks in months

Plan a month at a glance. Drag a post to a new slot and it moves on every network it belongs to. Week and day views when you need them closer.

02A copilot that knows your brand

Captions, hooks, full drafts, and multi-slide carousels in your tone, voice, and colors. The copilot reads your brand profile on every generation, and you review everything before it schedules.

03Automations that run themselves

A visual workflow builder: schedule triggers, RSS feeds, webhooks, conditions, and AI generation steps - all server-side. Set it once, let it post.

04Built for rosters, not just accounts

Workspaces, roles, and approval flows, so a freelancer or agency can run a roster of brands without spreadsheets. Plus a first-class MCP server and REST API, so your AI assistant can draft and schedule too.

How it works

Four steps to a full month.

1

Connect your channels

Link the networks you publish to. Twelve are supported natively - no workarounds, no "finish on your phone."

2

Set your brand profile

Tone, voice, language, and colors, once. The copilot reads it on every draft it writes for you.

3

Plan the month

Draft yourself or let the copilot start it. Tailor per network, drag everything into place on the calendar.

4

Approve and walk away

Review once, approve, and the studio publishes on schedule. Automations keep the routine posts flowing.
