## Pain inventory (verbatim)

P1 PAYWALL CREEP / free tier gutted:
"Free version is useless No matter what you need - you have to pay, $100/year is
serious money." (S1)
"It is just unbelievable how many features are locked behind their subscription. It
keeps getting worse and worse and even the most simple features are completely
unusable because you have to pay for them." (S1)
"More and more basic features disappearing. Have to pay to see all catches at one spot
now. Sad." (S1)
"Useless app now after update, unless you buy pro... your free version officially has
no features and is useless." (S1)
"approximately 40% express frustration over the app's extensive paywalls, which
restrict access to previously free features like viewing catches and maps" (S2)

P2 WRONG DATA / hallucinated catches:
"This app is telling me that in mountain stream so small that its not visible on map
was caught Yellowfin tuna, Snapper and Kahawai. Don't waste your time or money with
this." (S1) - THE source of the "yellowfin tuna in a mountain stream" line in the
builder brief; it is a real App Store review.
"Over 50% of all the catches in my area are wrong. How I know this, is cause those
species don't exist in those bodies of water or let alone this far north." (S1)

P3 BILLING TRAPS:
"I was charged $80 without warning and have struggled to get a refund, which is very
disappointing." (S2)
"I messaged them through the app to request that my account be cancelled to avoid the
yearly subscription charge on 8th July, but I heard no response." (S1)
"Around 15% of users report issues with unexpected charges and difficulties in
obtaining refunds." (S2)

P4 SPOT BURNING / location privacy:
Fishbrain itself acknowledges being "referred to as 'that spot burning app' in some
circles" and that "when Fishbrain first launched, many many years ago, it didn't have
privacy options." (S3 - the company wrote a whole blog post to fight the reputation)
"I wanted to use this as a personal log for fishing, but it's priotizing being a
social media platform for some reason and there's apparently no option for your
profile or logged ca[tches to be private]." (S9 - snippet provenance, flagged)

P5 VALUE EXTRACTION (users make the data, get charged to see it):
"They are utilizing information that is provide by users to create content and then
charging a premium to get it back. Not impressed." (S1)
"Fishbrain is overpriced and I refuse to keep adding value to this app for free, the
insistence on paid features, despite us already providing valuable data on fishing
locations and trends, is exploitative and unfair." (S1)

P6 REGULATIONS CONFUSION (Texas):
"Slot limits are one of the most misunderstood fishing regulations in Texas" - "If
you've ever measured a fish and thought, 'Wait... is this one legal?' - you're not
alone." Slot limits vary lake by lake. (S5)
"So if I buy an annual resident all water pack on Aug 15th, does it go into effect
Sept 1st or is it valid from date of sale...? Site is clear as mud." (S6)

P7 GEAR COST + DECISION OVERWHELM:
"Kayaks run 300$ to 3,000$ plus rigging." (S7)
"I've been contemplating purchasing a fishing kayak for a while. I've viewed more
YouTube Kayak reviews and online searches than you can imagine." (S8)

P8 CROWD DATA DOESN'T TRANSLATE TO CATCHING:
"half the time I did not catch anything or when I did it was a one off. I would see
people smoke a pond and then go there and get nothing." (S4 - paying premium user)

P9 TOXIC / GATEKEEPING COMMUNITY:
"a lot of rude people who can't handle being wrong" (S1)
"some of the people on here are quite homophobic/ transphobic and racist" (S1)
"y'all are like taking the fish too professionally and seriously. made me don't feel
like posting my catch at all." (S1)
