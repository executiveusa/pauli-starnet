# Refero Styles - design-system reference for the gate

- URL: https://styles.refero.design/
- Filed: 2026-09-27 by Jeremy's watcher, on his word: "this is a site they can crawl to reason over design systems."
- Owner: Creative District design gate (Darya Designs + Synthia Superdesign).

## What it is
A library of 2,000+ AI-readable design systems extracted from leading product websites. Each entry ("style") gives colors, typography, spacing and components as a ready-to-use DESIGN.md that can be dropped into Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, v0 or Lovable. Browsable by Trending / Popular / Newest with search. Beta.

## How the gate uses it
1. When a Brand World Contract needs reference material, search the library for adjacent product styles.
2. Open a style, read its DESIGN.md, and reason over its tokens (color, type scale, spacing, components) - as inspiration for standards, never as a brand's identity ("Preserve standards. Forget style. Every brand earns its own world.").
3. Cite the style URL in the design brief when it informed a decision.
