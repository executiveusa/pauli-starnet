# Video Department

The video and animation house of the city - the 10th district. Owner agent: **MONTY**
(rename is one word from the owner). Seated in VIDEO STUDIO; the RENDER BAY building runs
the cloud lanes (RunPod + fal connector ports).

Everything animated or filmed belongs here: avatar stories (Yappyverse, Where's Pauli),
UGC ad creatives, gateway-skin 3D assets, product demos. The Creative District stays the
design gate; this department is the production floor.

## Gates (standing rules)

- Cloud-first: nothing renders or stores on the owner's laptop.
- Money: RunPod spend is prepaid-balance-only; every paid job on any lane waits for
  explicit per-job owner approval with the exact cost shown first.
- Publishing: nothing ships to any channel without owner approval of the exact artifact.
- Automations: OFF by default, deliberately invoked.
- Honesty: claims come from run receipts, files, or provider read-backs. Proven vs
  unproven is always stated. Empty slots are honest vacancies - the department does not
  invent agents or outputs.
