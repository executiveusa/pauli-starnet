# Tila — one wholesale client for Plantas Tila

One job: Plantas Tila (air plants, sold wholesale inside Mexico; plantastila.com and Mercado Libre) closes its first
paying wholesale client. Owner decision 2026-11-17: Tila is promoted to a core project and is the clearest path to a
client. Agent: `tila-scout` (revenue district, `districts/revenue/citizens.yaml`), judged by `king-doe`.

## Inputs
- The business, as recorded in the owner's chat: wholesale plants inside Mexico, organic only, no ad spend, domain
  plantastila.com, Ivette runs operations and takes the calls through her own Instinct. Ivette owns Tila; StarNet only
  finds buyers and drafts.
- The Tila dashboard's 20 leads, 13 contacts and 186 opportunities carry a seed marker: treat them as unverified and
  re-check each one before using it.
- Sales method: `districts/pauli/board/seats/king-doe.md` (discovery before pitch, sell the outcome, list objections first).

## Process
1. Find buyers in Mexico who buy plants in volume: nurseries (viveros), florists, garden centers, event and wedding
   decorators, hotels and restaurants with living decor, gift and terrarium shops, corporate gifting. Public sources only.
2. Score each one 1 to 5 on: buys plants in volume; sells or uses air plants or similar; reachable by phone, WhatsApp or
   email from a public listing; inside Tila's delivery area. Keep 4+ only.
3. For the best lead each weekday, draft a short message in Spanish from Tila: who we are, one useful offer (a sample
   box or a first-order price), one question to start a conversation. No invented claims, stock or prices: leave
   `[precio]` and `[cantidad]` for Ivette.
4. Every draft waits for the owner's approval. Ivette (or her Instinct) sends approved messages and runs the calls.
5. When a buyer answers, hand the thread to Ivette the same day and log it.

## Outputs
- `output/leads.md`: one row per lead: name, city, type, source link, score, contact route, status.
- `output/drafts/`: one file per outreach draft, marked APPROVED or not.
- `output/EVIDENCE.md`: replies, calls, quotes and the first paid order (order or invoice number, date, amount).

## Done means
One paid wholesale order from a new client, recorded in `output/EVIDENCE.md` with its order or invoice number.

## Never
Send, call or post anything; promise prices, stock, shipping or delivery dates; ship live plants outside Mexico; spend
money. Live-plant shipping rules are unverified, so offer pickup or local delivery until Ivette confirms a carrier.

## Human check
The owner approves every outreach draft. Ivette confirms each price, quantity and delivery promise before it is sent.
