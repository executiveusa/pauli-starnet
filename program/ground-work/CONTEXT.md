# Ground Work — managed Instinct agents for nonprofits (Bambú's personal offer)

One job: sell and deliver one repeatable offer to nonprofits and social-purpose companies: a personal Instinct agent,
set up for one staff member, trained on that organization's own documents, with every outside action approved by a
person. It earns money for Bambú, funds New World Kids, and opens the door to serving nonprofits.
Owner decision 2026-11-17: this is the third project slot, next to MACS and Kupuri (Tila runs inside the Kupuri slot).

## Read in this order
1. `OFFER.md`: what we sell, to whom, the price and what is not included.
2. `DELIVERY.md`: the same 14 steps for every client (numbered stages, done-means, human check).
3. `STARTER-PROMPT.md`: the template each client pastes into their agent on day one.
4. `PATREON.md`: the membership tiers and page copy.

## Sources (what this is built from)
- UJIMA (`executiveusa/UJIMA`): the laws in `icm/shared/creative-operating-system/laws/` (nonprofit trust and ethics;
  growth, offer and retention), the readiness rubric (`docs/CLIENT-READINESS-SCORING-RUBRIC.md`) and the tenant
  stages (onboarding, opportunity scan, grants, campaigns, approval gate, outcome logging, learning).
  UJIMA's own servers, dashboard and live agent runtime are not finished (`docs/PRODUCTION-GAPS.md`), so this offer
  does not sell them. They're the upgrade path once proven.
- Ground Work: the nonprofit back-office service from the owner's earlier Claude sessions (videos with Nia). The
  film promises more than is delivered today; this offer sells only what `DELIVERY.md` can prove.
- The first real pilot (2026-11-17): one nonprofit staff member, ten minutes a day, an invite from nwkids.org and a
  personal starter prompt. That pilot is the model for every client.

## Rules
- The client owns their agent account, their documents and their data. We never copy client data into this repository
  (it is public) or into StarNet. Results are recorded here only as anonymized counts and dates.
- Nothing is sent, shared, spent or committed on the client's behalf without their approval (UJIMA's approval gate).
- No youth records, health details, home addresses or donor financials go into an agent (UJIMA nonprofit law 3).
- Every claim in a sale needs proof (UJIMA growth law 4). No invented testimonials or results.

## Outputs
- `output/PIPELINE.md`: each prospect, fit score, stage, next step (organization names only with their consent;
  otherwise "Org A", "Org B").
- `output/EVIDENCE.md`: per client: start date, tasks done, minutes saved per week (client-reported), paid or not.

## Done means (first 30 days)
3 paid Ground Work setups, recorded in `output/EVIDENCE.md` with invoice numbers.

## Human check
Bambú approves every outreach message, proposal and price quote. The client approves every outside action of their
agent.
