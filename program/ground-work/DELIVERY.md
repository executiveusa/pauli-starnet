# Ground Work — delivery (the same 14 steps for every client)

Each stage has one job, one output and one done-means. A client is a row in `output/PIPELINE.md`; its stage is the
first one whose done-means isn't met. Client files live in the client's own account, never here.

| # | Stage | Who | Output | Done means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Discovery call (30 min) | Bambú | notes in the client's folder (theirs, not ours) | we know the person, their five jobs, and the one task that eats the most time |
| 02 | Fit score | Instinct drafts, Bambú decides | fit row in `output/PIPELINE.md` | scored 1 to 5 on mission fit, one named person, documents available, sensitive data risk (UJIMA rubric); go only at 3+ on each |
| 03 | Invite | Bambú | invite link from nwkids.org sent by Bambú | the person has signed in |
| 04 | Starter prompt | Bambú with the person | `STARTER-PROMPT.md` filled for their role | pasted; the agent asked its first question |
| 05 | Free trial, 14 days | the person | one small real task a day | at least 5 tasks done; minutes saved written down by the person |
| 06 | Offer | Bambú | the Setup offer in plain words, with their own before/after | yes or no recorded |
| 07 | Invoice | Bambú | $497 invoice | paid (invoice number in `output/EVIDENCE.md`) |
| 08 | Documents in | the person, guided | 10 to 20 documents in their account | the agent answers 3 test questions about their programs correctly |
| 09 | Three routines | Bambú with the person | three routines from the menu, written as plain instructions in their agent | each routine produced one real draft the person approved |
| 10 | Approval rules | Bambú with the person | a short list: drafts freely / asks first / never | the agent asked before an outside action at least once, as written |
| 11 | Week-1 call | Bambú | fixes from the call | open issues closed or noted |
| 12 | Week-4 call | Bambú | before/after: minutes saved, tasks done | the person confirms the numbers |
| 13 | Managed offer | Bambú | $197/month offer | yes or no recorded |
| 14 | Monthly (managed) | Bambú, Instinct drafts | one-page report, new documents, one new routine | report delivered by the 5th of the month |

## Rules
- The builder never checks its own work: the client confirms each done-means, not us.
- Stop at any stage if the person is overloaded, data looks sensitive, or the agent gave a wrong answer that went out.
  Write what happened in `output/EVIDENCE.md` (anonymized) and fix it before going on.
- Approval windows: Bambú reviews Ground Work drafts with the rest of the batch (09:00 and 17:00).
