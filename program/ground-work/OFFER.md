# Ground Work — the offer

## In one sentence
A personal AI assistant for one person at your nonprofit, trained on your own documents, that helps with the
groundwork (drafts, reports, grant deadlines, thank-yous, follow-ups) in ten minutes a day, and never sends anything
without your OK.

## Who it's for
Small nonprofits, fiscally sponsored programs and social-purpose companies with 1 to 20 staff, where one person is
doing five jobs. Not for: organizations that want fully automatic posting or emailing, or that need the agent to handle
youth, health or financial records.

## The outcome we sell (not the technology)
Hours back every week for the person carrying the admin load, and fewer dropped balls: the grant deadline, the donor
thank-you, the board summary, the monthly report.

Routine menu: grant calendar and deadline reminders; first drafts of letters of inquiry and reports; donor and
volunteer thank-you drafts; board meeting notes to a one-page summary; weekly priorities check-in; social post drafts
in the organization's voice; monthly impact report draft from the person's notes.

Every client pays their agent provider (Instinct) directly; we don't resell it. Check Instinct's current price before
quoting, and confirm its terms allow us to help set up and support a client's own account.

## Objections (answer before they're asked)
- "We don't have time to learn AI." It's ten minutes a day, in a chat they already use; the agent asks one question at
  a time.
- "Is our data safe?" It stays in their own account. We never keep copies. Sensitive records never go in.
- "What if it sends something wrong?" It can't send anything without their OK.
- "We can't afford it." Start with the free 14 days; pay only if it saved real hours. Ask the funder: capacity-building
  grants often cover tools and training.

## Upgrade path (later, not sold today)
