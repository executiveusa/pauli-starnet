# Severity rubric (frozen v1)

- critical: active credential/private-boundary exposure, unauthorized spend/mutation, or false success causing imminent external harm.
- high: missing evidence on a consequential completion, repeated cross-boundary leak, or destructive watcher proposal.
- medium: stale heartbeat, watcher disagreement, non-durable state presented as durable, repeated retry/drift.
- low: isolated contract mismatch or attention debt without present harm.
- info: sourced observation with no correction required.

Deterministic rules assign severity. Synthesis may explain but not raise or lower it.
