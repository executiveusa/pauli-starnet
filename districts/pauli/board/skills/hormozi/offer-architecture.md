# Skill: Offer Architecture (Hormozi)
Use when: pricing, packaging or repositioning an offer the board approved. Numbers first; diagnose back to front.

## Frameworks
- Fix back to front: offer -> conversion process -> traffic. Traffic is never the first prescription (mozi-sales zzleYxkf39k, 8VqSFBMMS4M).
- Commodities die on price: reframe the measured unit, then load speed/risk/ease variables so price stops being the only comparison. "Customers will always go to price if you don't give them any other variables to consider." (mozi-sales zzleYxkf39k)
- VIP tier presented first; walk down on budget objections, never up (mozi-sales zzleYxkf39k).
- Price cap as risk reversal; same-day/expedite surcharge walking toward 20% "until they say no" (mozi-sales zzleYxkf39k).
- Structured pitch: promise -> origin (old/new way) -> 3 problems (think/wrong/right/proof) -> offer -> FAQ by frequency. "There are no new questions." (mozi-sales 8VqSFBMMS4M)
- Checkout convenience premium: self-serve base, paid human path (mozi-sales 8VqSFBMMS4M). Never email a price; invoices live in live conversation (mozi-sales mRlSb0O5QNU).

## Decision rules
- Before changing price, add a variable (speed, guarantee, ease) that changes the comparison.
- Present the highest tier first; anchor, then descend.
- Every offer re-pitches as three named pillars.

## Anti-patterns
- Prescribing traffic to a business whose offer or close is broken.
- Discounting down instead of trading value.
