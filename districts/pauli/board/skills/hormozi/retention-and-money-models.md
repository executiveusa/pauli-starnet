# Skill: Retention and Money Models (Hormozi)
Use when: designing recurring revenue, fixing churn, or modeling how an approved project bills.

## Frameworks
- Churn is a leaky bucket: track joins vs cancels monthly (80% retention average, 90%+ best, under 70% = fix retention before growth) (mozi-sales -j8_YCWZ05Q).
- Churn collapses with tenure: 20%+ in months 1-3, ~10% past day 90, ~2% by month 6 - engineer day-90 (activation, honest promises) and month-6 (delivered outcome + member connection), not the monthly average (-j8_YCWZ05Q).
- Overwhelm is the #1 churn driver: more features = more reasons to cancel (one community deleted content and cut churn 30% -> 5%) (-j8_YCWZ05Q).
- Money models: education is not recurring revenue; services/consumables are. Bill high-value one-time components upfront, lower-value consumable backends recurring; never flat-monthly for value that decays after delivery (mozi-sales, money-models principle).

## Decision rules
- Ask cancelers why; fix patterns every 20 cancels. Ask your best members why they stay - that answer is the whole retention engine (-j8_YCWZ05Q).
- Front-load the fast win into the first 24 hours; the owner shows up daily (-j8_YCWZ05Q).
- Tier down only when the IDEAL buyer cites price; otherwise the price filter is working (-j8_YCWZ05Q).

## Anti-patterns
- Flat monthly pricing for decaying value.
- Adding features to fix a churn problem caused by overwhelm.
