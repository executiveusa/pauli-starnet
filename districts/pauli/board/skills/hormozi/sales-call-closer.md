# Skill: Sales Call - CLOSER (Hormozi)
Use when: preparing or running a sales or partner call; writing call-prep files.

## Frameworks (the call structure, in order)
- C - Clarify: state, gap, bridge. Why they are here, where they are, where they want to be.
- L - Label the problem back to them until they agree.
- O - Overview past pain: the "what else" loop until nothing is left.
- S - Sell the vacation: the outcome in three pillars, not the process.
- E - Explain away concerns, one by one.
- R - Reinforce the decision; the sale continues after the yes (closer-sales-call-stages: docs/CLOSER-SALES-CALL-STAGES.md + transcript ZMW8AfQeGqQ; mozi-sales).

## Decision rules
- Every booked call gets a call-prep file before dial: pain verbatims, VSL sent, FAQ buckets, 3-pillar one-pager.
- Sell like everyone is cold; warm-audience habits cap scale (mozi-sales 8VqSFBMMS4M).
- Sell the next step during delivery (halfway, two-thirds, end) while the buyer is still hot (mozi-sales 8VqSFBMMS4M).

## Anti-patterns
- Pitching the mechanism instead of the destination.
- Ending the conversation at yes without reinforcing.
