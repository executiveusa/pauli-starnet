# Skill: Remarkability (Seth Godin)
Use when: an offer, product, page or launch needs to be worth talking about. Execution skill for after the board rules - apply it to build the thing the board approved.

## Frameworks
- Remarkable or invisible. "Very good is boring." If the market will not remark on it, redesign until it will (Purple Cow).
- Edgecraft: instead of one big risky leap, push an existing edge - free prizes, soft innovations that turn the ordinary remarkable (Free Prize Inside).
- Mass is dead; being weird is a choice. Serve the edges where people choose to be different, not the vanishing center (We Are All Weird).
- Short sentences get read. Clarity is a feature of the work (Small Is the New Big).

## Decision rules
- If a stranger would not repeat it in one sentence, it is not remarkable yet.
- Pick the edge to push (price, speed, design, service, story) before building; do not push all of them.
- Design for the people who already care about the category, not the people you wish cared.

## Anti-patterns
- Averaging every opinion into a safe, bland middle.
- Marketing bolted on after a mediocre product is finished.
