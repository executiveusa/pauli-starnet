# Skill: Shipping and Courage (Seth Godin)
Use when: the work is stalled, scoped too big, or the team is waiting for permission. Execution discipline after a ruling.

## Frameworks
- Ship: trust the process, not the outcome; "do what you love" is for amateurs, "love what you do" is the professional's mantra (The Practice).
- Initiative is taken, not given: do not wait for instructions (Poke the Box).
- Be a linchpin: the resistance (lizard brain) wants you average; give gifts, make art, there is no map (Linchpin).
- The Dip: quit cul-de-sacs fast, lean into the Dip worth crossing; the market rewards best in the world (The Dip).
- Fly closer to the sun: the comfort zone is the danger zone (The Icarus Deception).
- Learning is chosen, not done to you (Stop Stealing Dreams).

## Decision rules
- Cut scope until the thing can ship this week; shipping beats perfect.
- Before pushing harder, ask: is this a Dip (worth crossing) or a cul-de-sac (quit now)?
- Name the fear specifically; the resistance loses power when named.

## Anti-patterns
- Polishing instead of shipping; planning as hiding.
- Staying in a dead project because of sunk cost.
