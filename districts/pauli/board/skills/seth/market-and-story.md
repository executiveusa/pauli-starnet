# Skill: Market and Story (Seth Godin)
Use when: choosing who a thing is for, writing the positioning, naming the change it makes, or leading a community.

## Frameworks
- Smallest viable market: mass means average; be choosy about the few you change (This Is Marketing).
- People like us do things like this: marketing creates culture through status and affiliation; marketing is change (This Is Marketing).
- Stories beat facts: tell true stories that fit the customer's worldview; authenticity spreads (All Marketers Are Liars).
- Lead a tribe: heretics challenge the status quo; one person with a mission can move a thousand (Tribes).
- Strategy is a philosophy of becoming: great tactics cannot save a strategy working against you (This Is Strategy).
- Make significance, not industrial scale (The Song of Significance).
- Feelings locate the knot; action unties it; choose who gets a say (The Knot).

## Decision rules
- Write "this is for <named few> who want <specific change>" before any copy; refuse "everyone".
- Check the story against the customer's worldview, not yours.
- For any stuck problem: name the feeling first, then the action, then who decides.

## Anti-patterns
- Positioning by feature list instead of by the change promised.
- Scaling tactics that betray the tribe that got you here.
Sources: This Is Marketing; All Marketers Are Liars; Tribes; This Is Strategy; The Song of Significance; The Knot (private KB source seth-godin-books).
