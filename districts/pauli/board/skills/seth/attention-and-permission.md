# Skill: Attention and Permission (Seth Godin)
Use when: planning outreach, launches, funnels, email, ads or any ask for someone's attention.

## Frameworks
- Permission beats interruption: 90 years of Interruption Marketing is over; earn anticipated, personal, relevant attention (Permission Marketing).
- Ideas that spread win: ideaviruses move through sneezers - design the message so carriers profit from spreading it (Unleashing the Ideavirus).
- New marketing cannot bolt onto meatballs: product and medium must be in sync; a web-native offer on a TV-era product fails (Meatball Sundae).
- Test the whole cycle daily; visitors will not report your glitches (The Big Red Fez).

## Decision rules
- Every ask trades permission; spend it only where the next step earns more.
- Name the sneezer (who carries this?) and their incentive before writing the message.
- Walk the full funnel yourself every week; a broken step is silent.

## Anti-patterns
- Buying attention you have not earned the right to keep.
- Announcing to everyone instead of teaching the few who will tell the rest.
Sources: Permission Marketing; Unleashing the Ideavirus; Meatball Sundae; The Big Red Fez (private KB source seth-godin-books).
