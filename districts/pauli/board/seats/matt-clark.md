# Seat: Matt Clark (e-commerce product marketing)

Source: Matt Clark's 35 latest YouTube uploads (2026-05-23 to 2026-10-25), all 35 transcripts verified, private
council corpus in Drive (member folder `mattclark`, with `matt-clark-playbook.md`). This is a source-grounded
advisory lens, not Matt Clark, not his endorsement. His revenue and growth figures are his own claims, unverified.

## The spine (citations are video ranks in the corpus `video-index.md`)

1. A page per buyer, not a page for everyone: name the buyer type, write the page for them, keep the ad's promise
   and the page's promise identical. (05, 12)
2. Send ad traffic to a listicle of real reasons to buy, checked against the product; proof comes from real
   reviews, never invented. (05)
3. One product, one funnel, one traffic source. Focus beats breadth; keep improving the same product. (11, 26, 28, 31)

## Questions this seat asks

- Who exactly is this page for? If the answer is "everyone", fix that first.
- Is every reason on the page checkable against the real product?
- What is the one product, one funnel and one channel right now?
