## The spine (what everything hangs on)

1. **Sell booked jobs to Main Street, not "AI".** Boring local businesses
   (roofers, septic, waterproofing, chimney, med spa, dental implants) have big
   tickets, urgency, and bad websites. They don't want agents to manage; they
   want more of their existing traffic converted. (-JienW-k_1o, 4Ao8XPvWQbQ)
2. **Price on the job's worth.** ~$2.5-5k install + ~$1.5k/mo. High-ticket
   clients make the math work; a $50/mo product never does. (-JienW-k_1o,
   4Ao8XPvWQbQ)
3. **The funnel IS the proposal.** Build it in the prospect's brand, send a
   Loom, offer 24-48h deploy. First one free as a case study, then charge.
   (-JienW-k_1o)
4. **Audit before pitch.** Scrape ~100 businesses/city (Maps, licensing boards,
   ad libraries), score each site for gaps: phone + booking path, price-first
   step, email capture. His Charlotte sample: 9/11 no email capture, 8/11 no
   price-first step, 0 had traffic problems. (-JienW-k_1o, 4Ao8XPvWQbQ)
5. **Write at 5th grade in the customer's words.** "Water in my basement", not
   "hydrostatic remediation". (-JienW-k_1o)

## The 4 levels of vibe marketing (his operating ladder)

1. Prompts (ask better questions, feed competitor/gap analysis),
2. MCP-connected data (GA, SEO, ads accounts as knowledge sources),
3. Workflow automation (Gumloop/Zapier MCP for repeatable flows),
4. Full agents (socials agent, SEO machine). Record yourself doing a task with
   Loom, then ask the LLM how to automate it. (xfdc4E2gc70, 9EctdwjOBog)
