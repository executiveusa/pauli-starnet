# Four gates

1. **Identity/account:** Bambú approves account, channel and phone/MFA actions.
2. **Money:** Bambú approves exact price, renewal and cancellation terms.
3. **Representation/client:** Bambú approves any external/client words, recipient and promise.
4. **Publication/mission:** Bambú approves the final video, title, thumbnail, description, audience and timing together.
