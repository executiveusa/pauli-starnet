# Hook structure

Draft four lines, then compress:
- Proof: credible evidence relevant to this promise.
- Promise: the result or question, in the same language as the title.
- Plan: the shortest roadmap needed.
- Persona: the intended viewer and stakes.

Reject: biography first, generic greetings, delayed premise, unsupported revenue flex, repeating title/thumbnail without adding tension, or a promise the body cannot prove.

Evidence rule: Proof must be independently supportable. A creator's income, client-result, performance, or causal claim stays labeled as a creator claim and cannot serve as verified proof by itself.
