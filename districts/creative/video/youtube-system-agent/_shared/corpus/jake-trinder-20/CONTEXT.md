# Jake Trinder 20-video teaching corpus

Purpose: evidence shelf for the methodology. Read `manifest.tsv` to route to a source, `source-notes.md` for each video's description/chapters, and `evidence-map.md` for distilled creator claims. Do not load all source notes during production. Production reads the approved methodology instead.

The channel exposed exactly 20 public videos when captured September 15, 2026: https://www.youtube.com/@jaketrinder/videos . Metadata and descriptions were captured that day. At 6:23 AM CST, all 20 English transcript tracks were pulled with `youtube-transcript-api`, producing about 1.1 MB of raw timestamped text. Each transcript was matched to its manifest video ID, title, URL, and listed duration. These transcripts verify what Jake said in the videos. They do not independently verify income, client-result, performance, or causal claims.
