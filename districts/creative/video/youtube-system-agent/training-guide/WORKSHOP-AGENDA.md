# Team workshop - 90 minutes

1. 10 min - route four requests through the graph.
2. 15 min - diagnose examples by demand, choice, consumption, conversion.
3. 15 min - deconstruct one outlier without copying it.
4. 15 min - write title, thumbnail brief and Proof/Promise/Plan/Persona hook.
5. 15 min - compare client and faceless workflow differences.
6. 10 min - run rights, evidence and four-gate checks.
7. 10 min - complete the walk test and assign sandbox runs.

The workshop ends with files: orientation checks, methodology tests and assigned sandbox briefs.
