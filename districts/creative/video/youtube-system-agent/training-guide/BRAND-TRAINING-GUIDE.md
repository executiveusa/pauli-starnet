# YouTube System - brand training guide

## 1. What we are

We build YouTube as compounding media and business infrastructure. We are not social media experts guessing trends. We run a visible decision system: research demand, win choice, satisfy consumption, design conversion, measure the bottleneck, improve one thing.

## 2. How the same agent does different work

The request chooses the folder. The folder chooses the context. The agent does not carry one giant prompt.

- Methodology changes → `workflows/01_methodology-maintenance/`
- Client/business channel → `workflows/02_client-channel/`
- Faceless channel → `workflows/03_faceless-channel/`
- Agent/team training → `workflows/04_brand-training/`

The common philosophy lives once in `_shared/methodology/`. Working drafts never go there.

## 3. The operating sequence

### Demand
Know the viewer and their urgent language. Find what they already search and watch. Map outliers and weak supply. Record sources and a causal hypothesis.

### Choice
Write the title promise first. Design a thumbnail that adds information. Judge the pair against real competing options.

### Consumption
Open with Proof, Promise, Plan, Persona as briefly as possible. Deliver useful depth in a clear progression. Visuals prove or orient, not decorate.

### Conversion
Choose one next step matched to the workflow: lead asset, next video, subscriber, product, affiliate, or other approved goal. Do not scatter attention across seven CTAs.

## 4. The workflow for people

Each role works from a stage output, not verbal memory:

- Strategist: brief, demand map, idea and outcome.
- Researcher: source ledger, outlier decomposition, claim map.
- Creative director: title/thumbnail system and visual logic.
- Writer: original script that delivers the promise.
- Producer/editor: evidence-backed master, captions and rights ledger.
- QA: fact, rights, brand, disclosure, accessibility and link checks.
- Owner: exact account/money/client/publication approvals.
- Analyst: performance review and one next test.

One person may hold several roles. The artifacts and gates do not disappear.

## 5. Faceless standard

Faceless does not mean authorless. A passing faceless video has original research, an explicit point of view, distinct narration, rights-cleared visual storytelling, meaningful variation, a truthful package and an editorial review. Generic templated scene loops fail.

## 6. Evidence and claims

Creator case studies and income claims are hypotheses unless independently supported. Every load-bearing factual claim links to a live source. Every client result states its measurement basis. The team says "proven," "estimated," or "unknown" accurately.

## 7. Gates

The agent may prepare. Bambú retains identity/account, money, external/client representation and final publication decisions. Approval covers the exact final state, not a nearby version.

## 8. Walk test for every operator

From the root, the operator must route a request with at most two extra reads, name exact stage inputs and outputs, state status by scanning output folders, distinguish factory from product, and identify the next human gate. If they need oral explanation, the structure failed.

## 9. Certification

Certification is per workflow. Learners pass by producing and defending artifacts, scoring at least 17/20, with no missing governance category. Watching the 20 training videos alone does not certify anyone.

## 10. Manager rhythm

- Per run: approve stage boundaries, not every keystroke.
- Weekly: review pipeline status, bottlenecks, rights incidents, publish decisions and business outcomes.
- Monthly: send durable evidence through methodology maintenance; revise the shared philosophy only through an approved diff.
