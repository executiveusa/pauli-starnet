# Governance

- No public upload or publish without Bambú reviewing channel, title, thumbnail, description, video and schedule together.
- No new account/channel without Bambú approving the identity; phone/MFA taps remain his.
- No spend or credit use without amount, renewal terms, and cancellation state approved.
- No client-facing send, promise, or approval without exact words and recipient approved.
- No unlicensed media, cloned voice, copied script, deceptive synthetic person, or unverified claim.
- AI-assisted output must remain original, varied, useful, and human-edited to avoid repetitive/mass-produced content risk.
