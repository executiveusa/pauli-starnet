## Higgsfield Genjutsu (new video model, per the tutorial)

Two capabilities other models in our lane do not have:

1. **Motion transfer** - keeps the motion, camera and timing of a source clip, rebuilds the person/place/look from up to 30 reference images. Clips 4-30s, up to 1080p.
2. **Object swap** - changes one thing in a video and keeps everything else identical. No full regeneration. This is the cheap-fix tool: one bad element no longer costs a re-render.

Access paths: ChatGPT desktop Codex + Higgsfield plugin (MCP), or a Higgsfield API key (pay-per-use; the tutorial claims 50% off launch pricing). Pay-per-use fits our existing Higgsfield API lane - route spend through it, free lanes first, owner approval before any paid run.

## Patterns to fold in

- **Editing pipeline**: hyperframes + a personal "reel engine" skill pack - the LLM cuts ums/pauses/retakes, adds B-roll, transitions and motion graphics in the house style. This validates Hyperframe Station and extends it: the reel-engine skill pack is the missing house-style layer.
- **Avatar swap**: swap the on-camera person for an avatar plus a different voice. Sellable as variations of winning client ads - cheap, fast, trend-jacking as a service.
- **Copyright hygiene (hard rule for trend recreation)**: strip the audio BEFORE motion transfer, then re-slice the audio after. Source-clip music is how recreates get flagged.
- **Prompting pattern that works**: name the exact model per step, give ordered numbered steps ("first remove audio, then upload, then re-slice"), and end with a goal-locking line ("that is your goal") so the agent loops until done.
