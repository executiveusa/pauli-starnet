# Hyperframe Station DESIGN

## Style Prompt
Cinematic luxury reveal. Dark canvas, single brand mark earns the light,
wordmark follows, one accent rule, one line of copy. Restraint over spectacle.

## Colors (per-brand, from brand.config.json)
- bg: near-black canvas (Crown #0a0908, Kupuri #0b0f1a)
- ink: warm/cool off-white per brand
- accent: brand metal/signature (Crown gold #c9a86a, Kupuri chaquira blue #29abe2)
- accent2: secondary brand color (Kupuri purple #7b5ea7)

## Typography
- Luxury/legacy brands: Cormorant Garamond (display serif, italic tagline)
- Modern/craft brands: Montserrat light, wide tracking (Kupuri)

## What NOT to Do
- No long intros blocking content (Collins motion rejection list)
- No motion without a job (hierarchy/orientation/feedback/pacing/expression)
- No generic gradients, glassmorphism, or stock textures (cliche blacklist)
- No agent-invented taglines - placeholder until owner words
- No more than one accent rule and one tagline line
