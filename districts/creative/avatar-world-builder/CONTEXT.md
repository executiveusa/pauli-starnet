# Avatar World Builder Context

## Outcome

Give a human clear, compatible choices, then create a reproducible scene manifest—not an opaque one-off page.

## Non-negotiable gates

- Use only slots defined by the parts catalog.
- Do not spend credits, use a paid provider, or publish a deployment without the authority recorded in the release approval.
- No asset may enter a product without a license ledger: source URL, author, license, commercial status, attribution, derivative terms, version/hash, and approver.
- Offer choices before material creative commitments. Keep rejected options in the decision record.
- Treat web, mobile, reduced motion, and non-WebGL fallback as separate acceptance targets.

## Release posture

The owner has pre-approved Avatar World Builder logic and normal progression through its documented gates. This does not waive licensing, credential, paid-provider, or production-publish controls.
