# Pre-release copy check

- One real audience and one action per email; genuine consent and applicable compliance pathway confirmed.
- Subject and preview match body; no false urgency, spoofed replies, or fabricated relationship.
- Offer, price, link, eligibility, deadline, stock and proof checked against live client sources.
- Customer stories/testimonials approved for use; source attribution kept in internal ledger.
- Plain language and accessible mobile layout; brand voice not replaced by the source creator's style.
- Sequence trigger, timing, suppression, frequency and unsubscribe mechanics reviewed by authorized operator.
- Draft, approval, test hypothesis and metrics preserved; publisher has final approval for audience and words together.
