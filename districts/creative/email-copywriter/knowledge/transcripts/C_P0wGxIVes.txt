Have you ever made this mistake writing copy? Check this out. A lot of copy that I see nowadays has vague statements like this. Many clients or my car or more revenue or fast and really it needs to be hyper-specific which increases the believability of what you're saying. When you say many clients, say 103 clients, okay? Don't say my car, say my '69 red Camaro, okay? Don't say more revenue, say $15,459 in revenue, okay? Be really specific with it. Don't say fast, say in 60 seconds, okay? You want to be as specific as possible in everything that you write because specificity increases believability and the more somebody believes your copy, the more they buy from your copy.
