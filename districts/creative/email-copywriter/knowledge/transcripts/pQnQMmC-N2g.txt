One of the best books you could ever read, Scientific Advertising written by the Godfather himself, Claude Hopkins. In fact, one of my favorite lines from Scientific Advertising is this, "The product itself should be its own best salesman." I don't know what it is, but in 2026, people keep shipping crap products and they wonder why they don't sell. They're writing great copy and they're doing this and adding deadlines and urgency and all this stuff. Ship a better product. Copy is dead unless it is propelling a great product. The great Gary Bencivenga says, "A mighty product is better than a mighty pen." Meaning, you can't out copy a bad product. So, stop writing copy for a crappy product. Write a great product and the copy will write itself. That is a hack.
