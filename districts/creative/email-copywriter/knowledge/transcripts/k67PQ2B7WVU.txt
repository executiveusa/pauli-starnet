Stop using cold DMs to try and get your first copywriting client because one, you don't have any proof that you're good at what you're doing, so they're not going to buy. And two, if you were good, you wouldn't be cold DMing. That's the perception at least. So try this instead. Start writing on a platform like Twitter or LinkedIn in a hyperspecific niche. Post content every single day for 90 days. Tag the people that you wish you could work with and start a natural conversation with them. Get in their DMs after you've started talking to them and then sell them your services or work for free until you can build a portfolio. Organic content and relationships is the way to grow in 2025.
