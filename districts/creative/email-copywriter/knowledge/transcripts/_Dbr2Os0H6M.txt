Here are three dead-simple ways you can do market research as a copywriter. Tip number one is Reddit threads. Find subreddits in your niche category or topic, and then look at the posts that people are doing. What problems do they have? What questions are they asking? Figure out how your product can solve those problems for them, and then position your copy in that manner using their own verbage. Second tip is YouTube comments. Find videos in your relative niche category, whatever it is, even from competitors. Watch those videos, and then look at the comments. What did people like? What did they not like? What do they wish they had more of? And then take that and use it in your actual copy. Tip number three is Amazon reviews. Find books or anything like that in your category or niche, and then read the reviews, five stars and one stars. What did they like? What did they not like? What problems? Find the reoccurring themes across all those channels, write your copy that way.
