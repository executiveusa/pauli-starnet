Here are five ways to write irresistible headlines, subject lines, and hooks. Number one, curiosity. Make it a mystery of what they're about to watch. For example, here's a subject line that I wrote. This one food replaces eight supplements. What one food? Number two, crazy. Write something that nobody else is writing. For example, Google is trying to murder email marketers. What? Number three, challenge. Challenge existing dogma. Take something that they believe and flip it on its head. Like this one, why firing your clients will make you more money. Number four is culture. Use something culturally relevant like 50 shades of copywriting. And number five, credibility. Borrow somebody else's authority. For example, Tim Ferriss's number one tip to grow your newsletter.
