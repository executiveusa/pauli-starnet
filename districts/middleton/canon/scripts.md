# Verbatim scripts (cited per source video)

## Review ask (raffle)
"Hey, we would really value your feedback and if you give it to us, we will put your name into a raffle where you have a chance to win a membership for free for the year." [fBGbgabk1iE, YhgpaOENHik]

## Referral follow-up
"Thank you for the review. If you have any friends or family that would like to come in with you, we have got a couple 7-day passes. Anyone that comes from you gets your name into that raffle five more times." [fBGbgabk1iE]

## Reactivation SMS (example, med spa)
"We are reopening your consultation spot at Glow Aesthetic Studio with $100 off your first Botox or filler appointment, but only if you book by Friday." [TovKT7FEsF8]

## Elevator pitch
"Optimizing your digital storefront, optimizing your reviews and referrals, and making sure that everyone that is interested knows about you, hears about you, and when they come through, they get followed up with right away - so that you can focus on giving them a great experience and the system can focus on getting them through the door." [ZOupcRem_bM]

## Booking frame (pre-call)
"I will be able to tell really early on, probably in the first 30 minutes, just based off what we go over and review and the audits I pull between now and then whether or not I can help you out." [ZOupcRem_bM]

## Gap recap before price
"You said earlier you currently do not have a process to get reviews and you recognize that it is impacting your business negatively... without having a process in place to get reviews consistently, any marketing you do is only going to generate a fraction of what it could." [vmZL39DrbZE]

## Close
"The next thing we are going to do is process payment, sign the agreement, and then book you in for the onboarding so we can get you started." [vmZL39DrbZE]

## No-guarantee counter
"I do not offer guarantees because they then think it is a one-sided relationship. Let us lock in at this price for the next 3 months." [ZOupcRem_bM area]

## Discovery bank
CRM? website visits/opt-ins last 30 days? price per customer per month? revenue last 30 days? who builds your website - niche specialists? review process? referral program? staff follow-up on trials? last agency - did they get you reviews? [sysr3_FmEmk, PfhikUzfjDg, ZOupcRem_bM]
