# Middleton District

One-sentence law: **The district runs the method, it does not improvise.** Every asset, script, and experiment traces to canon (the extracted Middleton system) - script for script.

## What this district owns
- The canon: `districts/middleton/canon/system.json` - the full extracted system from 48 JP Middleton videos (2026): the 7-service sequence, offer structure, pricing, sales process, verbatim scripts, metrics, niche strategy, lead-gen methods, anti-patterns.
- The scripts bench: `canon/scripts.md` (verbatim, cited per source video).
- The experiment loop: `experiments/loop.md` + `experiments/log.jsonl` - the district proposes and runs bounded experiments against the method on its own, receipts standard.
- The gap-audit pattern: market-gap audit (served / partially-served / unserved matrix) as a repeatable pre-sales job.

## The method in one breath
Conversion website -> AI reactivations -> reviews/referrals engine -> instant lead nurturing -> AI receptionist -> AI sales coach -> paid marketing LAST. One end-to-end system, sold month-to-month per location, sequenced so every step fixes what happens closer to the money.

## Boundaries
- Canon is truth: anything the district produces cites canon or is flagged as an experiment result.
- No outbound contact: no SMS/email/DM to real leads without the owner approving the exact audience + wording. Drafts and audits are fine.
- Money gate: experiments propose; nothing spends or ships without the owner.
- Free floor first: research/drafting on free-tier models; paid lanes surface to the owner first.
- Separate branch until the owner says wire it: this district lives on `district/middleton-method`, unmerged.
