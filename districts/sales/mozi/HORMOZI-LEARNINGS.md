## The spine (what everything hangs on)

1. **Fix back to front.** Offer -> conversion process -> traffic. Every broken business gets diagnosed in that order; traffic is never the first prescription. (zzleYxkf39k, 8VqSFBMMS4M)
2. **CLOSER is the call structure.** Clarify (state/gap/bridge) -> Label -> Overview past pain ("what else" loop) -> Sell the vacation (3 pillars) -> Explain away concerns -> Reinforce the decision. (ZMW8AfQeGqQ)
3. **Commodities die on price.** Reframe the measured unit, then load speed/risk/ease variables so price stops being the only comparison. "Customers will always go to price if you don't give them any other variables to consider." (zzleYxkf39k)
4. **Sell like everyone is cold.** Warm-audience habits cap scale; cold-proof pitches convert both. (8VqSFBMMS4M)
5. **The sale continues after the yes.** Reinforce the decision, then sell the next step during delivery (halfway, two-thirds, end) while the buyer is still hot. (8VqSFBMMS4M)

## The money-models principle (offer lane handoff, same day)

Education is not recurring revenue; services/consumables are. Bill high-value one-time components upfront, lower-value consumable backends recurring; never flat-monthly for value that decays after delivery. Folded into the graph as `money-models-billing-match` and into offer-architecture step 7.

## The retention cluster (How to Get Your Customers to Stay FOREVER, -j8_YCWZ05Q)

Churn is a leaky bucket: track joins vs cancels monthly (80% retention average, 90%+ best, <70% = fix retention first). Churn collapses with tenure: 20%+ in months 1-3, ~10% past day 90, ~2% by month 6 - so engineer day-90 (activation, honest promises) and month-6 (delivered outcome + member connection) instead of obsessing over the monthly average. Ask cancelers why, fix patterns every 20 cancels. Ask your best members why they stay - that answer is the whole retention engine; subtract everything else. Overwhelm is the #1 churn driver: more features = more reasons to cancel (one community deleted content and cut churn 30%->5%). Tier down only when the IDEAL buyer cites price; otherwise the price filter is working. Front-load the fast win into the first 24 hours, and the owner shows up daily.
