# Call Prep Playbook

Owner: El Papo (pipeline ops). Every booked call gets this prep file before the agent dials. Nothing here is a script - it is the ammunition the agent reasons with.

## T-minus checklist

- [ ] Pain inventory attached: 5+ buyer verbatims for this avatar (Reddit/forums/DM log/reviews) - `pain-first-research`
- [ ] VSL sent for this conversation type (`vsl-before-call`): first-call VSL, renewal VSL, or rebook VSL. Call assumes it was watched.
- [ ] FAQ buckets refreshed from the real question log, sorted by frequency (`faq-by-frequency`): top question ~40%, second ~25%
- [ ] Offer one-pager: 3 pillars named, VIP + standard tiers, guarantee language, price-cap language
- [ ] Seeds-of-doubt questions loaded for the 2 most likely competitor comparisons
- [ ] Billing shape checked against value shape (`money-models-billing-match`)
- [ ] Reinforcement plan drafted BEFORE the call (next-steps sequence to deliver on the yes - `closer-reinforce`)

## CLOSER question bank (discovery)

- Clarify: "What made you take this call?" / "Where are you now, where do you want to be?" -> label the gap in their words; position as the bridge.
- Label: name the problem back to them plainly.
- Overview past pain: "What have you tried?" then "What else?" until the inventory runs dry (`pain-cycle-loop`). Associate with what they liked; disassociate from what they hated.
- Sell the vacation: the 3 pillars, tied to their stated gap, not to features.
- Explain away concerns: sort each concern - circumstances, other people, or fear of deciding. Answer, then ask for the decision.
- Reinforce: on yes, immediately walk the exact next steps and dates.

## Hard rules on the call

- Price is never emailed (`never-email-invoice`).
- Top tier presented first (`start-high-tiering`).
- Sell like the buyer is cold even if they are warm (`sell-to-cold`).
- Every unanswered "what else" becomes an objection later - empty the loop.
