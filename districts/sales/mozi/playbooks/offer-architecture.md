# Offer Architecture Playbook

Owner: Stackman (offer architect seat). Manager: Mozi. Spine nodes: `back-to-front-fix`, `offer-value-variables`, `break-commodity-frame`, `three-pillar-pitch`, `money-models-billing-match`.

## The build sequence (same for every product - only the product changes)

1. **Pain inventory first.** Buyer verbatims in hand (`pain-first-research`). No pain inventory, no offer work.
2. **One buyer, one outcome.** Name the single avatar and the dream outcome in their words.
3. **Break the commodity frame.** If buyers compare on one measured unit (hourly, per-seat, per-month), reframe the unit (per job, per outcome). Ref: `break-commodity-frame` - "Customers will always go to price if you don't give them any other variables to consider" (zzleYxkf39k).
4. **Load the value variables.** For each of speed / risk / ease, add at least one component:
   - Speed: expedite lane with surcharge (`speed-premium`: start 10%, walk toward 20%, "keep moving up until they say no")
   - Risk: price cap (`price-cap-risk-reversal`), insurance/proof components, `internal-certification-levels` (Level 3 = N+ jobs done)
   - Ease: done-for-you components, `false-offer` opt-out inclusions
5. **Tier it.** VIP tier + standard tier. Always presented top-first (`start-high-tiering`). VIP can be the existing service renamed plus one cheap component.
6. **Three pillars.** Chunk the whole offer into exactly three named pillars (`three-pillar-pitch`). This is the pitch unit.
7. **Match billing to value shape** (`money-models-billing-match`): high-value one-time components billed upfront; consumable/service backend billed recurring; never flat-monthly for value that decays after delivery; renewals default to `opt-out-billing`.
8. **Guarantee aimed at the hidden objector** (`guarantee-reframe`).
9. **Log the experiment.** Every offer choice becomes a named A/B test (`ab-test-designs.md`).

## Seeds-of-doubt bank (build per product)

For each competitor comparison the buyer will run, pre-build the question that surfaces the hidden cost: "Did the other guys give you a cap?" / "What insurance do the other guys carry?" / "Who actually shows up - employees or day labor?" Questions, never attacks (`seeds-of-doubt`, zzleYxkf39k).

## Convenience pricing

Self-serve checkout at base; human-hand-hold path at +premium (`checkout-convenience-premium`: $2,500 checkout vs $3,000 with 1:1, 8VqSFBMMS4M).
