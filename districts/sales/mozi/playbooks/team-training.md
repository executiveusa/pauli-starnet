# Team Training Playbook - Coach Ice Kenezzer's drill program

Owner: Coach Ice Kenezzer (Kronk Gym lineage - sales is trained like boxing: rounds, drills, film study). Audience: Bambu's non-technical team and the specialist agents. Everything trains off the same graph - no separate canon.

## Weekly structure (45 min, voice-first, WhatsApp-friendly)

1. **Film study (10m):** one mined case from the corpus, read aloud. Starters: the moving-company VIP tier case (zzleYxkf39k), the webinar-restructure case (8VqSFBMMS4M), the VSL-before-call case (mRlSb0O5QNU).
2. **Framework drill (15m):** one node per week, spoken reps. Week 1: `back-to-front-fix` (diagnose three real businesses back-to-front). Week 2: `pain-cycle-loop` ("what else" until empty). Week 3: `three-pillar-pitch` (chunk three different products into pillars). Week 4: `seeds-of-doubt` (write five competitor questions).
3. **Sparring (15m):** roleplay a CLOSER call. Capitan Cubano plays the objecting buyer (he is impossible to break); trainee runs the spine; Owl scores against the checklist.
4. **Test review (5m):** Taishan reads the experiment ledger - what won, what lost, what changes.

## Certification levels (internal-certification-levels applied to ourselves)

- Level 1: can recite the spine + run a discovery call with the question bank.
- Level 2: 25 live discovery calls logged + runs a full CLOSER call unaided.
- Level 3 (master closer): 100 calls + mentors a Level 1 + owns one experiment to a decision.

## For agents, not just humans

Specialist agents drill the same way: generated practice calls scored by Owl against node citations. An agent that pitches without a pain inventory fails the round, same as a human.
