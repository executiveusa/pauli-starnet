# Scripts and Frames Playbook - language patterns, not scripts

Owner: Mozi. QA: The Owl. Rule: agents generate situation-specific language from these frames; nobody reads fixed lines. Every generated line cites its node id. Verbatim quotes below are calibration anchors (how Hormozi actually phrases it) - never pasted into client assets.

## Opening a pitch (structured-pitch-architecture, 8VqSFBMMS4M)

Frame: BIG PROMISE -> WHO AM I (old way/new way origin) -> 3 PROBLEMS (what they think > why wrong > what's right > proof) -> OFFER -> FAQ BY FREQUENCY.
Anchor: "There's going to be a big promise right before that... this is the old way, new way... the big three biggest concerns... then you just systematically break down each of the three problems."

## Breaking price anchoring (break-commodity-frame, zzleYxkf39k)

Frame: refuse the measured-unit comparison, then load other variables.
Anchor: "We don't do it by hourly. We just do it by job." + "Customers will always go to price if you don't give them any other variables to consider."

## Presenting tiers (start-high-tiering, zzleYxkf39k)

Frame: top tier first, budget walk-down ready.
Anchor: "I always start high... as soon as someone says, 'No, I'm on a budget.' You're like, 'No worries, then just do standard.'"

## Seeds of doubt (seeds-of-doubt, zzleYxkf39k)

Frame: innocent question about competitor's hidden cost + pause.
Anchor: "Did the other guys give you a cap?" ... "Oh. Well, yeah, that's how they're going to get you."

## The upsell during delivery (sell-during-delivery, 8VqSFBMMS4M)

Frame: ask at halfway, two-thirds, end; keep the party going, not a new decision.
Anchor: "You want to sell when they're mid-decision, not making a new decision... 'Hey, let's just keep the party going.'"

## Handling the sales-averse founder (good-selling-feels-good, 8VqSFBMMS4M)

Frame: their own happy purchases were engineered too.
Anchor: "People only feel sold to if it's bad. A good selling feels good."

## Presenting price (never-email-invoice, mRlSb0O5QNU)

Frame: price only lives in live conversation.
Anchor: "Never present an invoice via email. It should always be over the phone because they're going to have questions."

## Guarantee (guarantee-reframe, 8VqSFBMMS4M)

Frame: short no-sweat window aimed at the absent decision-blocker, naming the absurd objection.
Anchor: "If he says [he doesn't want the outcome], no worries, just let me know refund you."

## CLOSER call spine (closer-framework, shorts:ZMW8AfQeGqQ)

Clarify (current state / desired state / gap / you are the bridge) -> Label the problem -> Overview past pain ("what else" loop) -> Sell the vacation (3 pillars) -> Explain away concerns -> Reinforce the decision.
