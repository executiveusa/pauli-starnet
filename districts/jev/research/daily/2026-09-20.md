### Idea 1: Dead-SKU Repricing Trigger

JEV decides which dead inventory gets repriced, held, or liquidated.

**Typed questions JEV answers:**
- `is_sku_dead` (confidence 0–1)
- `reprice_action` (hold / discount / liquidate)
- `recheck_after_days` (integer)

**Money lane:** Recovered margin from dead stock + avoided storage fees.

**Cost-to-try:** Free floor — use existing sales data + a spreadsheet for shadow repricing.

**Smallest proof mission:** Take 10 dead SKUs. Run JEV shadow decisions. Apply to 5, hold 5 as control. Measure sell-through in 14 days.

### Idea 2: Ticket Triage + Refund Decision

JEV routes inbound support tickets and decides auto-refund vs human.

**Typed questions JEV answers:**
- `intent_class` (billing / technical / abuse)
- `refund_eligible` (boolean, confidence 0–1)
- `action` (auto-refund / human / deny)

**Money lane:** CS labor cost per ticket avoided + faster resolution.

**Cost-to-try:** Free floor — pull 100 historical tickets, simulate JEV decisions.

**Smallest proof mission:** Compare JEV decisions to human outcomes. Target ≥90% agreement, 100% fail-safe on high-value tickets.

### Idea 3: Ad Creative Kill/Scale Gate

JEV decides which ad creatives get scaled, killed, or tested next.

**Typed questions JEV answers:**
- `beats_control` (confidence 0–1)
- `budget_action` (scale / hold / kill)
- `next_test_variant` (string)

**Money lane:** Ad spend efficiency — ROAS lift by killing losers early.

**Cost-to-try:** Free floor — use past campaign performance data.

**Smallest proof mission:** 20 past creatives. Shadow-decision vs human scaling. Measure if JEV would have avoided at least one losing spend.
