### Idea 1: Ad Spend Kill Switch

JEV monitors live ad sets and decides whether to kill, hold, or scale — before the daily budget burns.

**Typed questions JEV answers:**
- `is_ad_set_underperforming` (confidence 0–1)
- `budget_action` (kill / hold / scale_1.5x)
- `recheck_after_hours` (integer)

**Money lane:** Saved ad spend on losers + faster reallocation to winners. Even $200/day saved across 5 ad sets = $3K/mo recovered.

**Cost-to-try:** Free floor — Meta/Google Ads export CSV + JEV shadow decisions in a spreadsheet. No API spend needed for proof.

**Smallest proof mission:** Pick 5 active ad sets. Run JEV shadow kill/hold/scale calls daily for 7 days. Compare JEV's calls against what you actually did. Measure: would JEV have saved spend without killing winners?

### Idea 2: Lead Score + Instant-Quote Gate

JEV scores inbound leads on first touch and decides: fire instant auto-quote, queue for human, or discard.

**Typed questions JEV answers:**
- `lead_quality_score` (0–1)
- `route_action` (auto_quote / human_queue / discard)
- `quote_amount_tier` (low / mid / high / custom)

**Money lane:** Faster response = higher close rate. Auto-quoting qualified leads at 2am that currently wait until morning. Even 2 extra closes/mo at $500 margin pays for the SKU.

**Cost-to-try:** Free floor — forward lead form submissions to a JEV endpoint that returns typed decisions; log to sheet. No human changes yet.

**Smallest proof mission:** Run 20 historical leads through JEV. Compare its route_action vs what humans actually did. Measure: would auto-quote on the top-tier leads have saved time without misrouting junk?

### Idea 3: Subscription Churn Intercept

JEV scans active subscriber behavior and decides which accounts get a retention offer, which offer type, and which to let walk.

**Typed questions JEV answers:**
- `churn_risk_score` (0–1)
- `retention_offer` (none / discount_20 / pause_30d / upgrade_incentive / human_save)
- `offer_timing` (immediate / next_billing_cycle / triggered_by_event)

**Money lane:** Each saved subscriber at $299/mo = $3,588/yr retained. Saving 3/month = ~$10K ARR protected.

**Cost-to-try:** Free floor — export last 90 days of subscriber activity + churn outcomes. Run JEV shadow scoring on the data. No live offers sent without owner approval.

**Smallest proof mission:** Score 30 accounts that already churned. Did JEV flag them as high-risk before the cancellation event? Then score 30 active accounts — how many does it flag for immediate intervention? Owner reviews the list before any offer ships.
