### Idea 1 – Micro‑Transaction Fraud Guard  
**Typed questions JEV answers**  
- `transaction_risk_score` (confidence 0‑1 that the purchase is fraudulent)  
- `block_now` (yes / no) – if **yes**, decline the transaction instantly; if **no**, let it proceed  
- `review_later` (yes / no) – if **yes**, flag the transaction for manual review after settlement  

**Money lane**  
Every avoided fraudulent charge saves the full transaction amount plus charge‑back fees (≈ $15 USD per dispute). For a mobile game processing $200 K/mo in IAP, a 0.3 % fraud‑rate reduction yields ~$600/mo recovered revenue and lowers dispute‑related overhead.  

**Cost‑to‑try (free floor)**  
Export the last 48 h of raw purchase logs from your payment gateway (fields: user‑id, amount, IP, device fingerprint, time‑of‑day, prior‑failed‑attempts) into CSV. Run JEV shadow classifications locally – no API calls needed.  

**Smallest proof mission**  
1. Shadow‑run JEV on the exported log.  
2. Compare JEV’s `block_now` decisions against the gateway’s actual fraud labels (available from dispute reports).  
3. Measure precision/recall and compute the estimated dollar value of prevented fraud.  
Success criterion: ≥ 0.8 precision on fraud cases with ≤ 5 % false‑positive rate, projecting a net‑gain > $300/mo at current volume.

### Idea 2 – Community‑Comment Moderation Assistant  
**Typed questions JEV answers**  
- `toxicity_level` (low / medium / high) – confidence‑weighted estimate that the comment violates community policy  
- `action` (allow / warn / remove) – recommended platform response  
- `escalate_to_human` (yes / no) – if **yes**, send to human moderator for final judgment  

**Money lane**  
Reducing manual review time cuts moderator labor cost; a 10 % reduction in reviewer hours on a team of 5 FTEs saves ≈ $4 K/mo (assuming $80 k annual fully‑loaded cost per FTE). Higher‑quality discourse also improves user retention, lifting ad‑impression CPM by ~0.2 % – worth ~$1 K/mo on a 5 M‑impression/day forum.  

**Cost‑to‑try (free floor)**  
Pull the last 7 days of public comments from your forum’s export (fields: comment‑id, user‑id, timestamp, raw text, any existing moderation tags). Store as CSV and run JEV shadow inference on a laptop.  

**Smallest proof mission**  
1. Run JEV on the comment dump, capturing `toxicity_level`, `action`, and `escalate_to_human`.  
2. Randomly sample 500 comments and have a senior moderator label the correct action (allow/warn/remove).  
3. Compute agreement (Cohen’s κ) between JEV’s recommended action (ignoring escalate flag) and the human label.  
Success criterion: κ ≥ 0.6 (substantial agreement) and ≤ 15 % of cases requiring human escalation, indicating the tool can safely handle the majority of moderation work.
